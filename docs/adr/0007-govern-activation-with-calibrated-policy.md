# Govern activation and rollback with calibrated policy

A0 Self-Improvement will govern both Improvement Artifact types through one immutable, versioned Activation Policy that preserves authority-ranked evidence, per-bucket uncertainty, a predeclared Candidate Benefit Claim, prospective canary evidence, exact-revision activation, bounded monitoring, and ancestry-safe rollback. Numerical boundaries have no authority until supported by an approved Policy Calibration Artifact; the built-in uncalibrated policy may enforce hard vetoes and run diagnostics but cannot authorize activation or soft statistical rollback. This favors explainable, false-promotion-resistant decisions over mutable thresholds, scalar scores, and rapid automatic activation.

## Consequences

- The Activation Policy is bound into the Assessment Profile, Evidence Bundle, Canary Trial, Activation Receipt, and Post-Promotion Monitor. A policy change stales dependent evidence and ends an active trial as inconclusive rather than reinterpreting it in place.
- Evidence reduction first verifies identity, authority, capability, fixtures, and shared harness validity; then candidate-attributable hard vetoes; complete deterministic bucket and family coverage; per-bucket noninferiority and declared benefit; optional calibrated blinded judgment; efficiency; prospective canary evidence; and the final Activation Coordinator CAS.
- Shared harness, availability, cleanup, fixture-authority, provenance, or identity failures yield review-only or incompatible evidence and never count against the candidate. Candidate-attributable safety, policy, secret, schema, live-tool, fixture-order, hard-budget, and protected-constraint violations reject regardless of judge output or aggregate quality. A passing candidate may repair a deterministic incumbent failure.
- Required buckets, Fixture Families, validators, protected cases, and contamination rules are declared before evaluation. Fixture Family is the sampling and resampling unit. Every required bucket must independently pass its one-sided policy boundary; missing, stale, contaminated-only, underpowered, availability-failed, or boundary-crossing evidence is review-only and cannot be averaged away.
- Promotion requires noninferiority in every required ordinary bucket, zero permitted protected regression, and credible improvement on one predeclared Candidate Benefit Claim. Indistinguishable candidates without declared benefit remain review-only.
- A blinded judge is soft evidence unless the policy explicitly requires it. Its exact model/revision, prompt, rubric, calibration set, and Judge Calibration Receipt are bound to the Assessment Profile; label mapping is hidden and randomized, passes are counterbalanced, and disagreement, abstention, malformed output, or stale calibration makes the judge unavailable. Self-reported judge confidence is not statistical confidence.
- Only a current promotion-ready candidate may enter an authoritative Canary Trial. A reason-coded, authorized Diagnostic Canary may examine a review-only candidate but cannot repair missing replay authority, contribute promotion evidence, or auto-promote. Rejected candidates cannot enter a normal trial, and force may bypass only operational scheduling gates.
- A Canary Trial preregisters eligibility strata, a trial-secret HMAC assignment policy, stable history-bearing Canary Exposure Units, arm allocation, required comparable counts by bucket, a fixed terminal horizon, expiry, and hard-stop rules. An Exposure Receipt is written before outcome observation. Replay, background, withdrawn, drifted, and ineligible activity is excluded, and observations never resume or mix across trials.
- Canary v1 continuously evaluates only authoritative hard vetoes and identity or envelope drift. Ordinary quality and reliability are evaluated once at the preregistered horizon. The Canary Conclusion is passed, failed, inconclusive, or stopped; underpowered expiry, shared failure, drift, cancellation, or boundary-crossing uncertainty is inconclusive, never a pass or candidate rejection.
- Every activation mode requires a passed authoritative canary. In manual-only mode the operator starts the trial and authorizes final activation; canary-required may start through an authorized workflow but awaits operator activation after a pass; auto-after-canary requires explicit per-context opt-in and may start and activate automatically after a pass. Manual authority never weakens evidence requirements.
- Immediately before activation, the Activation Coordinator revalidates the canary, candidate, evidence, grants, fixture eligibility, incumbent profile and exact scope revision, Execution and Assessment Profiles, policy, capability certificate, and monitor plan. One CAS selects the complete successor Activation Profile and emits an Activation Receipt. Conflict makes the candidate lineage-stale and is never automatically rebased.
- A Post-Promotion Monitor begins with activation and binds new observations to the exact compatible Monitor Reference from the passed canary and frozen replay. It retains no undisclosed predecessor traffic. Candidate-attributable hard vetoes trigger immediate idempotent rollback; ordinary regression can trigger automatic rollback only at preregistered looks under calibrated uncertainty. Availability or harness failure pauses or invalidates monitoring and never becomes candidate harm.
- Rollback creates a new Activation Scope revision and selects the exact compatible predecessor by default. Another previously active ancestor requires current schema/capability compatibility and explicit authority. Emergency operator rollback may bypass statistical trigger requirements but not coordinator ownership, target compatibility, expected revision, bounded reason, or audit receipt. Rollback never rewinds storage or revives promotion evidence.
- If no compatible rollback target exists, Safety Bypass makes the Runtime Composer apply no improvement artifacts while ordinary Agent Zero behavior continues. Required authority loss makes active evidence stale, blocks further automatic changes, and begins an Evidence Requalification Window; protected-authority loss requires rollback, while any temporary continuation for other authority loss must be explicitly policy-bounded and ends in rollback or a recorded blocked state if requalification fails.
- Candidate Risk Tier is standard, elevated, or restricted and is fixed before evidence is observed. Only explicitly opted-in standard candidates may use auto-after-canary; elevated candidates require manual final activation; restricted candidates remain diagnostic. Prohibited component changes are rejected rather than assigned a risk tier.
- The checked-in policy is initially uncalibrated. Existing case-count, confidence, quality, allocation, horizon, expiry, monitoring, and rollback numbers remain non-authoritative until an approved Policy Calibration Artifact establishes them. Legacy automatic intent migrates to canary-required plus an explicit warning and never silently opts a context into auto-after-canary; conflicting legacy values yield policy-unconfigured.
- Mutation APIs request coordinator transitions and return receipt identity, observed and resulting scope revisions, policy version, disposition or action, and allowlisted reason codes. Public projections expose only safe opaque identities, coverage, freshness, bounded rates or deltas, trial/monitor state, and rollback eligibility. Reducer, statistical, judge, canary, CAS, monitor, rollback, privacy, clean-framework, authorized-provider, and exact live-runtime acceptance remain separate gates.
