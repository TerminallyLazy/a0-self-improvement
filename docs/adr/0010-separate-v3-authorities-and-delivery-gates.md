# Separate v3 orchestration authorities and delivery evidence gates

A0 Self-Improvement v3 will use a fresh single-host Safe Projection Store with append-only typed domain records, links, and events; narrow single-writer coordination rows; one coordinator-owned activation lane; and killable fenced attempt processes that cannot write authoritative state. Public status will be a pure content-free read, operator commands will be revision-bound and receipt-bearing, and the WebUI will project independent authority axes instead of inventing lifecycle state. Delivery will proceed through thin Outside-In slices and independently proved source, compatibility, provider, review, release, marketplace, public-install, and exact-runtime gates. This favors recoverable authority, truthful operator state, and attributable evidence over mutable JSON blobs, mixed job/domain status, worker-owned publication, request-started supervision, and a single ambiguous definition of done.

## Consequences

- The v3 Safe Projection Store contains immutable typed records, canonical digest-covered link manifests with constrained query indexes, and append-only domain events. Its only mutable authorities are the context Activation Scope, current operation slots, Work Items, monotonic fences and leases, cumulative budget reservations, the command/idempotency ledger, and ephemeral worker heartbeats. Disposable projections are rebuildable and have no domain authority.
- The migration ledger and encrypted Privacy Quarantine remain outside the normal runtime store. Migration is operator-initiated, copy-on-write, checkpointed, and committed through a Store Authority Manifest CAS that embeds the exact Migration Receipt bytes, making authority and proof durable together. Runtime never silently falls back to raw v2 after cutover.
- Work Item state is limited to queued, leased, cancel_requested, completed, failed, and cancelled. Attempt Conclusion, Publication Result, Activation Disposition, Replay Arm Outcome, Canary Conclusion, and candidate lifecycle remain separate typed facts.
- The Work Coordinator alone owns enqueue, claim, lease, retry, cancellation, two-phase orphan recovery, fenced finalization, Attempt Conclusion receipts, and terminal Work Item state. A child Work Attempt receives frozen inputs and no writable database handle. The Worker Supervisor kills its process group on cancellation, deadline, lease/fence loss, dependency drift, grant loss, or budget exhaustion and reports exit/cleanup facts only; the coordinator revalidates them before retry or publication.
- The Candidate Publication Planner is pure: it validates a staged result and builds one complete immutable write set. The Work Coordinator revalidates lease, fence, deadline, dependency, capability, grant, budget, incumbent, and scope revision and commits that write set with terminal Work Item state in one transaction. Late results remain undiscoverable.
- The Budget Broker reserves and reconciles every external call and metric dimension outside model libraries. Unknown post-crash usage stays unreconciled and cannot be treated as free.
- The Fixture Authority, Replay Pair Orchestrator, Evidence Reducer, and Activation Coordinator have distinct powers. Workers cannot activate; the Evidence Reducer cannot activate; API handlers cannot activate directly. Genesis, canary, activation, monitor, rollback, requalification, and Safety Bypass remain coordinator-owned exact-revision transitions.
- Canary start/conclusion, monitor conclusion, requalification, and operation-slot clearing each commit their immutable record/event with an exact slot CAS. At most one Canary Trial exists per context. An authoritative trial requires promotion_ready and calibrated policy; an explicitly authorized Diagnostic Canary may inspect review_only under a permanent no-promotion ceiling. Every candidate activation unconditionally requires an exact passed authoritative conclusion. Exposure identity is unique by trial plus exposure unit and is committed before observation. Activation never overwrites an occupied monitor/requalification slot, while rollback or Safety Bypass terminalizes every displaced slot in the same transaction.
- Runtime Composer is a pure read and Null Artifacts have no effect. Status remains observable while improvement is disabled and cannot initialize Genesis, migrate, repair, write caches, reconcile workers, or start processes.
- Public APIs expose opaque identities, stable schemas, independent capability/lineage/disposition/canary/monitor axes, freshness, and allowlisted reason codes. They omit raw content, prompt replacements, model reasoning, exception strings, filesystem paths, secrets, global hashes, and quarantine identities.
- Mutation commands require authentication, CSRF, context-scoped Operator Authority, exact expected revision, idempotency, bounded reason, and action-specific authority. Content authoring additionally requires a purpose-scoped Operator Content Session. A local-only Operator Authority Service explicitly bootstraps issuer identity and solely owns signed grant/session issuance, expiry, renewal of renewable grants, revocation, and receipts; content sessions are never renewed and require fresh local step-up. There is no implicit administrator or default grant. Framework admission owns auth/CSRF/method/transport; plugin pre-domain admission owns strict schema/context/authority/revision/idempotency equivalence. Failures at either layer create no mutation receipt. After complete admission, the action-owning coordinator commits the command-ledger row and Operator Mutation Receipt atomically with the accepted mutation or policy refusal; same-key/different-request is a receipt-free 409 conflict.
- The operator UI has six bounded areas: Overview; Candidates; Evidence and Fixtures; Privacy and Migration; Policy and Capabilities; and Receipts and Audit. It removes uncalibrated authority presets, mixed queue/candidate labels, raw active guidance, and silent empty-state fallbacks.
- Fixture authoring requires source attestation, strict content, safety checks, a live Fixture Use Grant, independent review, a Fixture Admission Receipt, family grouping, and deterministic partitioning. Quarantine release creates a new Released Fixture draft that must pass this same workflow. Authority-root, grant, quarantine, export, deletion, release, withdrawal, and cutover mutations use a local-only nonnetwork protocol; WebUI and HTTP can observe only content-free state and receipts.
- The breaking state-authority redesign is released as 2.0.0 after 2.0.0-rc.N validation. Release communication must distinguish pre-cutover recovery, post-cutover rollback, Privacy Quarantine export/deletion/release, Activation Profile rollback, and package downgrade.
- Implementation begins with the standalone package/test spine, strict schemas and inert Genesis, then privacy migration, work/fence/budget authority, fixture governance, certified replay, deterministic candidate flow, canary/activation/rollback, model routes and outcome-GEPA, operator UI, and calibrated automation.
- Every activation, including manual activation, and every soft statistical rollback remains unavailable until an approved environment-appropriate Policy Calibration Artifact exists. No provisional sample count, confidence interval, threshold, or UI preset becomes an authoritative default.
- Standalone source, standalone fake-provider behavior, clean pinned Agent Zero compatibility, authorized provider behavior, pull-request review and merge, release-candidate artifact, RC exact-runtime acceptance, final release, marketplace metadata, public-install identity, and final public-path exact-runtime acceptance are separate Delivery Evidence Gates. No gate is inferred from another.

## Evidence basis

- Current Store construction creates directories and performs migrations, so the status path is not a pure read: helpers/store.py, helpers/state.py, and api/status.py.
- Current queue state mixes pending/running with candidate/promoted/rejected, while optimizer and worker paths write overlapping candidate state outside one fenced publication transaction: helpers/store.py, helpers/queue.py, helpers/optimizer.py, and helpers/scheduler/worker.py.
- Current cancellation uses a ThreadPoolExecutor whose shutdown waits for the running future, so it cannot guarantee killed work or prevent late durable writes: helpers/scheduler/worker.py.
- Current prompt and guidance activation use separate pointers and mutation paths, contrary to the unified Activation Profile decision: helpers/prompt_artifacts.py, helpers/promotion.py, api/promote.py, and api/rollback.py.
- Current WebUI reads fields omitted by the safe status projection, conflates telemetry with quality gates, forces optimization, and installs unsupported numerical presets: webui/main.html, webui/dspy-rlm-store.js, and webui/config.html.
- The standalone test bootstrap imports usr.plugins.dspy_rlm and does not collect in a clean checkout; the repository has no package manifest or CI workflow: tests/conftest.py and tests/.
- The pinned Agent Zero provider-resolution contract accepts ModelType while the current plugin supplies a string, so compatibility must be certified rather than inferred: helpers/model_resolution.py in the plugin and helpers/providers.py in the pinned framework.
- ADRs 0001 through 0009 establish the unified profile, outcome, privacy, baseline, fixture, replay, activation, budget, dependency, schema, and engine-authority boundaries that this orchestration preserves.
